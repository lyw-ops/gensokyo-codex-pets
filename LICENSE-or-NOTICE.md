## Project identity

Gensokyo Codex Pets is an unofficial fan project based on Touhou Project. Touhou Project and its characters originate with Team Shanghai Alice / ZUN. This repository is not an official Touhou Project title and does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by Team Shanghai Alice, ZUN, OpenAI, or any referenced game's creators or publishers.

The project should be reviewed against the current [Touhou Project fan-creator guidelines (Japanese)](https://touhou-project.news/guideline/) and [English guidance](https://touhou-project.news/guidelines_en/) before distribution. Those pages can change; links here are references, not a substitute for reviewing the current terms.

## Asset policy

Repository sprite assets are intended to be original fan-made pixel art created for this project.

Do not commit:

- sprites, textures, or other game materials extracted from Touhou Project titles;
- sprites copied from Touhou LostWord, Touhou Mystia's Izakaya, or another commercial or licensed work;
- third-party fan art without the creator's permission;
- reference images whose redistribution rights have not been established.

Visual references may be documented as links and study notes. A reference does not grant permission to reproduce or redistribute its artwork.
